Bhutan is a small kingdom in the Eastern Himalayas. It is known for its
mountains, its forests and its monasteries. The country measures its progress
with Gross National Happiness instead of only looking at money. More than
half of the land is covered by forest, and the constitution says that at
least sixty percent must always stay forested. Thimphu is the capital city,
and Paro has the only international airport. Many students study computer
science at the College of Science and Technology, where they learn about
algorithms such as sorting, searching, dynamic programming and greedy
methods. Huffman coding is a greedy method: it always joins the two least
frequent symbols first, and this simple rule builds the best prefix code.
